The hardware

What actually goes on your house.

Four parts, and only one of them is visible from the street — barely.

A slim extruded aluminium channel, powder-coated to match your fascia in white, black, brown or bronze. It mounts tight under the soffit or along the roof edge and carries the whole run, so nothing is clipped, stapled or taped to your house.

Individually addressable RGB and warm-white diodes, seated inside the channel and facing down. You see the light they cast, not the bulb itself. Rated for 50,000 hours — about twenty years of normal evening use.

A weather-rated controller tucked somewhere discreet — usually beside the garage or the electrical panel. It joins your home Wi-Fi and is what your phone talks to, whether you are in the driveway or three time zones away.

Low-voltage, energy-efficient and plugged into a standard exterior outlet. Running the whole roofline costs pennies a night, and scheduling means it only runs when you want it to.

The whole fixture. Mounted, sealed and colour-matched — photographed in the rain.

The process

From first call to first switch-on.

Step 01

Free consultation

We come to you, walk the property, measure every roofline and accent run, and talk through what you want the house to feel like after dark. No charge and no pressure.

Step 02

Custom design & fixed quote

You get a layout drawn for your home's actual peaks, gables and accents, the track colour matched to your trim, and a written price that does not move afterwards.

Step 03

Professional installation

Our own trained technicians handle every ladder and every connection. Most single-family homes are finished in a single day, and we clean up behind ourselves.

Step 04

Setup, training & warranty

We join the controller to your Wi-Fi, build your first scenes alongside you, and hand over a 10-year parts-and-labour warranty before we leave.

The app

Total control. Zero complexity.

Everything below happens from your phone, from anywhere with a signal.

  • Zones — split the house into sections and colour each independently
  • Individual bulbs — address a single light for patterns, chases and gradients
  • Schedules — dusk-to-dawn, or a holiday that sets itself while you are away
  • Brightness — full brilliance for a party, a whisper for a quiet evening
  • Animations — fades, twinkles, sweeps and motion effects
  • 1,000+ presets — start from one, tweak it, save it, share it with neighbours
