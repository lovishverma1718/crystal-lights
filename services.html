Service 01

Permanent LED Lighting

Permanent, app-controlled outdoor LED lighting for year-round elegance. Installed once into a slim, colour-matched aluminium track that follows your roofline — then left up for good.

  • Individually addressable LEDs — every bulb is its own pixel
  • 16 million+ colours, plus dedicated warm and crisp whites
  • Rated for 50,000 hours, roughly 20 years of normal use
  • Track powder-coated to match white, black, brown or bronze trim
  • Weatherproof housings and sealed connections throughout
  • No annual setup, no takedown, nothing to store
Service 02

Seasonal Displays

Transform your home or business with festive lighting displays for Christmas, Halloween, Diwali and other special occasions — all from the same system you already have up.

  • Christmas reds and greens, or classic warm white icicle looks
  • Halloween oranges and purples with motion and flicker effects
  • Diwali golds and ambers, Canada Day red and white, Pride spectrums
  • Schedule a holiday in advance and let the house dress itself
  • Over 1,000 built-in presets — or build and save your own
