02 The invisible difference

Discreet by day.
Dazzling by night.

A slim, colour-matched aluminium track tucks under your soffit and follows the line of your roof. From the street, it reads as trim. After dark, it reads as magic.

  • Channel powder-coated to match your fascia — white, black, brown or bronze
  • Bulbs sit recessed and face down, so you see the glow, never the glare
  • Sealed, weather-rated hardware built for Pacific rain, snow and salt air
  • No clips, no staples, no seasonal holes in your soffit
