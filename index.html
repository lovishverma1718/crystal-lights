  • Bulbs sit recessed and face down, so you see the glow, never the glare
  • Sealed, weather-rated hardware built for Pacific rain, snow and salt air
  • No clips, no staples, no seasonal holes in your soffit

How it happens

From first call to first switch-on, usually inside two weeks.

Step 01

Free consultation

We walk your property, measure the roofline and talk through what you want the house to feel like. No charge, no pressure.

Step 02

Custom design

You get a layout drawn for your home's peaks, gables and accents, with the track colour matched to your fascia — plus a fixed quote.

Step 03

Professional install

Our trained technicians handle every ladder, fastener and connection. Most homes are finished in a single day, cleanly.

Step 04

Setup & training

We connect the system to your Wi-Fi, build your first scenes with you, and don't leave until you can drive it yourself.
