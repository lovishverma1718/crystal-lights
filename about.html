Our story

Every home deserves to shine year-round.

That belief is where this company started, and it is still the whole point of it.

Every December, thousands of British Columbians drag a box of tangled lights out of the garage, climb a wet ladder in the dark, and do it all again in reverse six weeks later. Half the strand does not work. The clips have gone brittle. Someone always ends up on the roof in the rain.

We built Crystal Lights to end that cycle for good. One professional installation, a slim track that disappears into your roofline, and a system that handles Christmas, Halloween, Diwali, Canada Day, birthdays and every ordinary Tuesday in between — from your phone.

We are a local team serving the Lower Mainland, Vancouver Island and the Okanagan. The people who quote your home are the people who install it, and the people who pick up the phone if you ever need us again.

Our own crew. Not subcontractors, not a franchise.

What we stand on

Built on trust and innovation.

01

Premium materials, always

Colour-matched aluminium track, sealed connections and commercial-grade LEDs. We do not have a cheaper tier, because we would not install one.

02

A design per property

No template layouts. Your peaks, your gables, your stonework and your sightlines get measured and planned individually.

Trained technicians only
